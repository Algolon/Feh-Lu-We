# Mushroom Grove R03 — restored checkpoint and nursery ecosystem

R03 adds a visible understory to the enlarged R02 grove. The 13 successful giants, four medium islands and 20 approach adults keep their geometry, positions and transforms. Around them are **183 sculpted young mushrooms** (220 specimens total), irregular families and nine additional edge/island colonies. Every giant has at least four young neighbours within 2.3 m, including a clearly visible medium-small companion. The young layer has no collision, and its centres stay at least 1.5 m from the approach centreline. The existing collision-driven return route remains open.

## Models, ground detail and reference comparison

Four nursery geometries, **728 triangles each**, retain the cap families: broad curled shelf, violet bell, mint lantern and slender rose spire. They use sculpted ring surfaces, root flare, curved/fluted stems, asymmetric cap edges, folded geometric lamellae and flattened irregular scales. A second deformation increases lean. Young specimens range from roughly 0.3 to 1.5 m overall height, with shared-base pairs, uneven fans and deliberate empty space. Their independent random stream preserves the R02 adults.

The two new references (`1792071000897.jpeg`, `1792070998102.jpeg`) were recovered and inspected. The implementation follows their size hierarchy, small companions at large stems, cyan/mint undersides and folded glow foliage. Screenshots show clearly separate cap silhouettes and visible stems from player eye height. The rendering remains the game's stylized low/mid-poly woodland style; the gray reference's fine sculptural engraving is represented by geometry/vertex painting rather than a high-resolution sculpt texture.

The young palette uses teal, mint and blue-violet with restrained berry accents. A per-instance emission gain creates bright companions and quieter buds; colonies pulse at different phases. Reduced motion freezes this. Folded mint ground leaves remain one merged draw. Half of the existing 64 spores now sit close to the ground; no extra particle draw or individual lamps were added. The two practical point lights remain unshadowed.

## Atmosphere, vision and sound

R02's 13 m clearing at plan (179,160), approach from (166,140), expanded fog envelope and stronger shader are retained. Wave amplitude is .0135, chromatic separation .024, with slow lens rotation/breathing, hue rotation, stronger single-pass highlight diffusion and coloured vignette. Depth fog darkens distant woodland while near paths stay readable. The normal direct render path remains outside the field. There is one colour/depth target and one fullscreen pass, four glow taps on low/eight on high, no history/trail buffer.

Scene fog, light clock, progression and save schema are unchanged. HTML UI remains outside the shader. Reduced motion disables displacement/chromatic separation and freezes shader time, light pulses and spores. The independent persistent setting **Dromerige schermvervorming** disables screen displacement while retaining the physical ecosystem.

The existing original Web Audio score remains: five detuned stereo pad voices, slow suspended/add9 harmony and sparse chimes with finite echoes. Zone gain crossfades against forest ambience through the existing master/mute route. Browser gesture unlock, pause and context handling are retained.
