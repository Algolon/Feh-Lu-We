## Proposed standards to test, not permanent production policy

**Rendering.** Retain Lambert/Phong as primary. Phong is sufficient for this selected pilot; do not convert the whole game to PBR. Selected MeshStandard families remain a hypothesis for roughness-sensitive hero metal/ceramic/stone, requiring a genuinely matched device/player-eye comparison. This test did not isolate a compelling roughness benefit for all families. Start with DPR 1.25 on capable phones, fallback 1.0, and permit 1.5 only after sustained p95/thermal tests. Do not default to 1.75/2.0. Anisotropy 4 is a bounded oblique-surface candidate, with device caps; this run does not establish a hardware cost win.

**Textures.** Make authored direction, restrained colour and deliberate scale standard principles, rather than this exact swatch set. Use 512px by default for tiling/small families; 1024px selectively when distance evidence shows a loss at 512. No evidence here justifies 2048. Start physical texel-density targets around 128–256 texels/m for architecture and 256–512 for selected furniture faces; these are authoring hypotheses, not a measured universal optimum. Check at actual phone distance, use distinct along/across grain scale and intentional end grain, avoid universal world-space wood. The current planar end-face mapping is an approximation, not a finished end-grain asset. Base colour is sRGB; data maps linear; keep mips. Prioritise coherent local values over noise. Paint/fabric replacement and small normal maps are deferred.

**KTX2.** Selective yes as a supported delivery experiment, broad migration deferred. The recovered 1024 ETC1S asset is 186,190 bytes and transcodes on this host to 1,398,128 bytes of BC7 mips versus a 5,592,405-byte RGBA+mip estimate. The comparison WebP is a 324,630-byte lossless decoded round-trip: it does not establish a win against optimized original lossy WebP, or fidelity against the missing source. Test ASTC/ETC2/RGBA fallback, startup/transcode, cache, licensing and thermal behaviour on phones before migration. Reuse Three's bundled matching decoder path and remove the redundant copied decoder from a future production integration. Do not require estate-wide conversion or resurrect unmeasured historical UASTC conclusions.

**Geometry/budgets.** Keep the historical 250k/150 guides as warnings, while testing a 350k camera-triangle view first on phone and 500k as a stress case. About 700k is optional diagnostic work, not a shipping target. High counts are justified by visible support construction, major silhouette and repeated reusable members that batch well; keep micro-decoration, invisible joins, foliage card tessellation and tiny all-edge bevels aggressively low. Track camera and shadow calls/triangles, alpha coverage, resolution, material families and texture bytes independently. A triangle cap cannot substitute for these measurements. No permanent DEV-04D budget changes are made.

**Code-first versus GLB.** Code-first modular construction proved sufficient for these six selected assets with small source growth and two added support batches. An authored GLB comparison was optional and was not performed; selected sculptural/machine/curved hero props are candidates, not demonstrated wins. Defer that genuine comparison of calls/loading/reuse/iteration before adopting it. Reject a mandatory Blender workflow. The runtime bevel helper is a calibration tool, not permission to bevel every piece.

**Visibility.** Architecture must survive while geometrically visible; hero landmarks need persistent distant silhouettes; room/portal visibility takes precedence for interior furniture. Keep existing portal occlusion. Remove obvious visible distance cutoffs for important architecture and cheap visible hero props selectively; do not set every estate object to infinite range. Native large-tree near/medium/far/off bands (16/26/48m, 3m hysteresis) should remain the starting point. Legacy vegetation 26/42/105m paths need matched silhouette/overdraw tests before migration. Start transition hysteresis around 3m for medium/large geometry, adjusted to movement speed and object scale. Small vegetation and micro-decoration can exit earlier when insignificant; projection should use actual shape/screen contribution, with an independent entry threshold. The current diagnostic fade re-enters partly transparent and ignores card foreshortening: DEFER, not a finished anti-popping solution. No new hero proxy earned adoption in the reconstructed run.

**Art direction.** Constructional believability, warm deep timber, restrained material identity, crisp silhouettes and readable contact depth strengthen Crafted Stylized European Woodland Manor. Preserve painterly simplification, scene hierarchy and coherent local colours. Do not adopt photoreal micro-noise, sharp normals on simplified forms, beige uniformity, neon vegetation or dramatic lighting that hides weak construction. The supplied reference qualities guide principles; this is not a literal realistic-style copy.

## Success and stopping boundary

At least one normal standing-distance view shows a substantial construction-read improvement without relying on close craft screenshots; costs are explicit. The wide Library improvement is modest, the full plaster experiment fails, and shadows/physical performance remain unresolved. This supports a **qualified successful calibration**, not a claim that the estate-wide quality gap has been closed. Owner judgement of the phone images and physical-device tests is the next decision.

The deliverable is the isolated branch and evidence. No merge, production deployment, global material substitution, gameplay/save/ID change, DEV-04D room rewrite or permanent budget revision is authorised by this recommendation. Stop for owner review.
