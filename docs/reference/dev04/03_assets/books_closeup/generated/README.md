## Allowed use
Modelling / stylization reference for:
- **shape language** — silhouette, proportion, how soft vs. firm the forms are;
- **material breakup** — where one material stops and the next starts, palette weight;
- **construction logic** — what supports what, how parts join and meet the ground/wall;
- **detail level** — how much ornament/detail Feh Lu We should carry at this scale.

## Not
- Not final concept art and not a layout: estate footprint, canonical IDs, collision and puzzle evidence win over anything in this image.
- Not a measured drawing: perspective, AI-generated detail — take proportions as intent, not dimensions.
- Low resolution (~500 px): use for read at first-person distance, not for texture sampling.

Contract for this subject: `../GENERATED_REFERENCE.md`; external refs in `../REFERENCES.md`.

## Use this sheet for
- Family construction: separate cover boards with visible overhang over a recessed paper block; rounded spines with raised bands.
- Size/thickness classes (small / medium / tall; thin / thick) and stacked arrangements derived from the same base family.
- Open-flat and open/reading variants with visible page curl and cover thickness (inset *Open book construction*).
- Page edges as a lighter, slightly uneven block — not a flat white face (inset *Page edges & paper material*).
- Cover-colour family: oxblood, moss, slate blue, brown, ochre, sage, parchment.

## Do not take from this sheet
- Gilt ornament density on every spine — at first-person shelf distance this becomes noise; use on a minority of hero books only.
- Readable titles/text — not needed and costs texture budget.
