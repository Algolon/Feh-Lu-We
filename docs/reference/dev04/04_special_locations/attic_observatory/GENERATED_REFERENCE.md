# Personalized reference — Attic observatory

Approved generated direction.

## Architecture
- Observatory is an actual usable attic nook integrated under the roof.
- Roof/attic partitions meet the sloped structure cleanly.
- Beams visibly support/organise the roof volume.
- Use the exterior-visible opening/window relationship; do not fake a telescope looking through a closed wall.
- No giant observatory dome or tower unless a later level decision explicitly adds one.

## Telescope
- Believable tube + mount + tripod/fork construction.
- Enough operator clearance around the instrument.
- Eye/sightline aligns with actual sky opening.
- Adjacent storage/furniture can make the attic feel used without blocking the viewing position.

DEV-04A may correct attic space/geometry; detailed telescope remodel is DEV-04B if not required for correctness.
